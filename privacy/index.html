壁纸照片分析器隐私政策

适用于 Google Play 版 Android 应用（com.awesomephoto）。开发者为 Cyeam。更新日期为 2026 年 11 月 24 日。English

照片与位置信息

应用读取你授权的照片，在设备上按日期、尺寸和评分筛选壁纸。扫描会读取照片尺寸和拍摄时间。你可以在 Android 设置中更改照片访问范围。若你在预览中授权读取照片位置，应用从原照片文件读取 GPS 坐标，并在系统地理编码服务可用时尝试解析地址。系统服务可能联网处理坐标。应用没有请求设备实时位置，也不会将照片、照片坐标或评分结果上传到 Cyeam 或 Firebase。

评分缓存和已下载模型保存在设备上。设置动态壁纸时，所选照片的副本保存在应用私有目录，不参与 Android 备份。导出时，副本保存到你选择的位置。Android 的备份和设备迁移设置可能适用于其他应用数据。

模型下载与诊断

首次分析及切换至未下载的模型时，应用通过 HTTPS 从 ModelScope 下载模型。服务方可能处理传输所需的 IP 地址和请求信息。下载请求只包含需要下载的模型信息。应用无需注册或登录，也没有广告。

应用使用 Google Firebase Analytics 和 Crashlytics 了解应用使用情况及修复故障。Google 可能处理使用与会话事件、设备和应用版本信息、应用安装标识符、崩溃堆栈及诊断信息。Google Analytics 可能从 IP 地址推断大致区域。本应用关闭广告 ID 采集和广告个性化信号。Google Firebase 隐私说明。

保留、删除与联系

清除应用数据或卸载可删除本机评分缓存、模型和动态壁纸副本。你导出到相册或其他位置的副本可在相应位置手动删除。Google 按相应服务的数据保留规则处理统计与诊断数据。Crashlytics 将崩溃堆栈及关联标识符保留 90 天后开始移除。Firebase 安装标识符保留到删除请求发出。Google Analytics 的用户级和事件级数据按属性的保留设置自动清理。

如需询问隐私事项或请求删除可定位的服务端数据，请发邮件至 lichao@cyeam.com。我们会核对请求，并在能确认归属时处理。联系时请勿发送原照片或照片位置。

Awesome Photo Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Android app distributed on Google Play (com.awesomephoto), developed by Cyeam. Updated November 24, 2026.

Photos and location metadata

Awesome Photo reads the photos you authorize and ranks wallpaper candidates on your device. Scanning reads image dimensions and capture dates. You can change photo access in Android settings. If you grant access to photo location in the preview, the app reads GPS coordinates embedded in the image. When available, Android’s geocoding service can resolve an address and may process the coordinates over the network. The app does not request your live device location or upload your photos, photo coordinates, or scores to Cyeam or Firebase.

Scores and downloaded models are stored on your device. When you set a live wallpaper, selected photo copies are stored in the app’s private directory and excluded from Android backup. An export saves copies to the location you choose. Android backup and device transfer settings may apply to other app data.

Model downloads and diagnostics

The app downloads missing models over HTTPS from ModelScope. The host may process the IP address and request information needed to deliver the files. Model requests identify the model to download. The app does not require an account and contains no ads.

Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics help Cyeam understand app usage and fix crashes. Google may process app usage and session events, app and device metadata, installation identifiers, crash stack traces, and diagnostics. Google Analytics may infer a general area from the IP address. The app disables advertising ID collection and ad personalization signals. See Firebase privacy and security.

Retention, deletion, and contact

To remove local scores, models, and live wallpaper copies, clear app storage or uninstall the app. You can delete exported copies from the location where you saved them. Google handles analytics and diagnostics under the relevant service retention rules. Crashlytics retains stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal. Firebase installation identifiers are retained until a deletion request is made. Google Analytics automatically removes user- and event-level data according to the property’s retention settings.

For privacy questions or to request deletion of data we can identify, email lichao@cyeam.com. We review requests and act on records we can verify as yours. Please avoid sharing original photos or photo coordinates in your email.
