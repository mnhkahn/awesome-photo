## Full description

Find your next wallpaper in the photos you already have. Pick a date range and Awesome Photo analyzes the gallery photos you allow it to access. It scores their aesthetics, subject clarity, and sharpness on your Android device.

- Browse ranked results and filter by score, subject, or orientation.
- Open any result to see how its score was calculated.
- Export the originals from your filtered results to a folder you choose.
- Use selected photos as a live wallpaper slideshow on supported devices, with a rotation interval and order you choose.

You’ll need an internet connection to download the models on first use. After that, photo analysis works offline. Your originals stay on your device and there’s no account to create.
